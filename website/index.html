The Problem We Tackle

One Scary Message. No One to Ask.

Your mother gets an SMS: “bank account blocked, verify now”. Your father gets a job offer asking for a registration fee. They panic — and every option available today fails them.

✕

Family WhatsApp Groups Bury It

An urgent screenshot drowns under good-morning forwards and birthday wishes. By the time someone replies, the money is gone.

✕

AI Chatbots Can't Be Trusted Blindly

A generic chatbot answers — but would you trust it with your parents' savings? There's no human you love confirming the decision.

✕

Google Needs Expertise

FamilyShield is built for this exact moment: AI explains it, family confirms it, and the decision gets recorded — before anyone clicks, pays, or shares an OTP.

FAQ

Questions Families Ask Us

Everything you need to know before bringing your family on board.

What exactly is FamilyShield?

FamilyShield is a private space where your family checks anything suspicious online. Paste a message, link, or screenshot — AI explains the risk in simple words, and your family confirms the decision together. Ask AI. Ask Family. Decide Safely.

How does a scam check work?

Submit the suspicious content and the AI returns a risk level (High, Medium, Low, or Unable to Assess) with the exact reasons why, plus safe next steps. If you're still unsure, send it to selected family members in one tap — they reply with one-tap verdicts and the final decision gets recorded on the case.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes. Ask in Hindi, Hinglish, or English — the AI answers in the same language you used, including the emergency guidance.

I already sent money / shared my OTP. What now?

Don't panic — act fast. FamilyShield immediately shows emergency guidance: call the toll-free cyber helpline 1930, report at cybercrime.gov.in within 24 hours, and ask your bank to freeze the transaction. Save every screenshot, SMS, and transaction ID as evidence.

Is my family's data private?

Yes. Families are invite-only via link or QR code. Each case is shared only with the members you choose, and you can delete your chats, cases, shares — even your whole account — at any time.

How do I get started?

Sign up with email or Google, create your family, and share the invite link or QR code. Members join with one click — no technical knowledge needed, which is exactly the point.

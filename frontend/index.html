How to use FamilyShield

1
Create or Join a Family

Start by creating a new family or join an existing one using an invite code. This is your private hub for staying connected.

2
Invite Family Members

Share your invite link or QR code with family members. As admin, you can manage roles and rename the family.

3
Open Family Chat

Use the Family Chat to communicate in real time. Messages are delivered instantly via WebSocket.

4
Ask Shield (AI Assistant)

Tap the purple "Ask Shield" button to get AI-powered safety advice, guidance, and answers to your questions.

5
Stay Notified

Enable push notifications to receive instant alerts. The bell icon shows unread notifications at a glance.

6
Manage Your Profile

Click your avatar in the top bar to edit your display name or change your password.
